At a glance

Dishcover does not sell personal information, show behaviorally targeted advertising, or use advertising trackers. It does not collect precise location, contacts, browsing history, or health data.

1Who we are and scope

Dishcover is a native iOS recipe application developed and operated by Kaan Şengün, an individual developer based in Türkiye (referred to as “Dishcover,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). For applicable data-protection law, Kaan Şengün is the controller of personal information handled through Dishcover.

This Privacy Policy applies to the Dishcover app, its custom backend, and these legal pages. It is a notice about our practices; it does not ask you to waive any privacy right. Third-party services may also process information under their own privacy terms, as explained below.

3How we use information

  • create, authenticate, secure, and maintain your Dishcover account;
  • store your profile, reviews, ratings, favorites, and followed cuisines or categories;
  • display and personalize recipe-related features you request;
  • process AskAI questions and return generated answers;
  • deliver opt-in re-engagement reminders and new-recipe or category alerts for cuisines you follow when push notifications become available;
  • verify Dishcover Pro subscription status and provide paid features when the subscription becomes available;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, enforce our Terms of Service, and protect users and the service;
  • diagnose crashes and improve reliability when planned crash reporting is enabled; and
  • comply with law, respond to lawful requests, and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising and do not build advertising profiles.

5Services, providers, and disclosures

We disclose only the information needed for the following recipients to perform their roles:

  • Google Firebase: Firebase Authentication supports email/password and federated sign-in; Cloud Firestore stores account, recipe, review, favorite, and preference records; Cloud Storage stores profile photos and recipe media; Firebase Cloud Messaging will support opt-in push notifications; and Firebase Crashlytics is planned for diagnostics. Google processes data under its applicable Firebase terms and privacy documentation.
  • Google and Apple sign-in: Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple confirms your identity and provides the account information you authorize. Google and Apple separately govern their own sign-in services.
  • AskAI providers: Our custom backend sends the text of an AskAI question to Google Gemini and/or OpenAI to generate a recipe-related response. The selected provider receives the query text and technical information necessary to return the response. Avoid entering secrets, health information, or other sensitive data.
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit: Apple processes Dishcover Pro purchases, renewals, cancellations, refunds, and payment details. Dishcover receives only the information needed to determine subscription entitlement or restore a purchase.
  • Authorities or involved parties: We may disclose information if reasonably necessary to comply with law or legal process, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud or security issues, or address a business reorganization. If control of Dishcover changes, we will require the recipient to handle personal information consistently with this policy and applicable law.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not permit service providers to use Dishcover information for their own advertising.

6How long we retain information

We retain personal information only while it is reasonably necessary for the purpose described in this policy, including providing the service, protecting it, resolving disputes, and meeting legal obligations. The criteria below guide our retention:

  • Account and profile information: for the life of the account, then deleted or de-identified after an account-deletion request unless a limited record is required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or a legal claim.
  • Reviews, ratings, favorites, preferences, and uploaded media: until you remove them, delete your account, or request deletion, subject to limited backup retention and legal requirements.
  • AskAI queries and responses: only as long as needed to process and operate the feature, troubleshoot it, protect against misuse, and meet legal obligations. AI providers may retain data under the service terms that apply to our backend.
  • Push token: until notifications are disabled, the token is replaced or becomes invalid, or the account is deleted.
  • Subscription status: while needed to provide or restore entitlement and for any legally required transaction, accounting, or dispute record. Apple retains the underlying purchase record under its own policies.
  • Crash diagnostics: for the period configured in Firebase Crashlytics and no longer than reasonably needed to diagnose trends, improve stability, and protect the app.

Deletion from active systems may not be immediate where information remains in encrypted or access-controlled backups. Backup copies are isolated from ordinary use and are deleted or overwritten through the normal backup cycle. We may retain de-identified information that cannot reasonably identify you.

7Your choices and controls

  • Profile and content: update available profile fields and manage reviews, ratings, favorites, and followed preferences in the app.
  • Push notifications: choose whether to allow notifications when iOS asks. You can later change the permission in iOS Settings. Disabling notifications stops delivery but does not delete your account.
  • Camera and photos: grant or deny access through the iOS permission prompt and change it later in iOS Settings.
  • Subscriptions: manage or cancel Dishcover Pro through your Apple Account subscription settings. Dishcover does not control Apple billing.
  • Account deletion: request deletion through Profile → Share Feedback. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

These rights may be limited where an exception applies, and we will explain a denial when the law requires.

Where required, transfers are supported by recognized safeguards such as adequacy decisions, approved standard contractual clauses, or another lawful transfer mechanism. 11Camera and Photo Library

Dishcover requests camera or Photo Library access only when you choose to set or change your profile photo. iOS displays the permission request, and you decide whether to allow access. If you select a photo, the selected image is uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage and associated with your profile.

Denying access does not prevent you from using unrelated app features. You can review or change permissions in iOS Settings. Dishcover does not scan your photo library or access photos you have not selected through the system picker or granted permission to use.

12Children’s privacy

Dishcover is not directed to children under 13, and children under 13 may not create or use an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact us through the method below so we can investigate and delete it where required. A higher minimum age may apply where local law requires it.

13Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect personal information. These include authenticated access, transport encryption, Firebase security controls and access rules, access limited to what is needed to operate the service, and service monitoring and updates. Authentication credentials are handled by Firebase Auth and sign-in providers, and payment details are handled by Apple.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and respond to suspected incidents as required by law.

If a change materially affects your rights or how personal information is used, we will provide additional notice in the app or through another appropriate channel before the change takes effect when required.
